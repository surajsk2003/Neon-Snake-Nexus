# 🐍 SPIRITED SNAKE - A GHIBLI ADVENTURE 🌸

A magical reimagining of the classic Snake game inspired by Studio Ghibli films. Guide a forest spirit through an enchanted meadow in this beautifully crafted, whimsical adventure built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.

## ✨ Features

*   **Studio Ghibli Aesthetics:** Soft, dreamy visuals with floating cherry blossom petals, gentle clouds, and magical particle effects
*   **Forest Spirit Theme:** Play as a mystical forest spirit with organic, flowing design and magical eyes
*   **Spirit Energy System:** Manage your spirit energy that gradually decreases over time - find special magical fruits to restore it
*   **Magical Food System:**
    *   🌺 **Regular Fruits:** Pink magical berries that grow your spirit and increase score
    *   ⭐ **Special Star Fruits:** Rare purple star-shaped fruits that appear temporarily and provide bonus energy
*   **Dynamic Particle Effects:** Beautiful magical particles when eating food and leveling up
*   **Floating Environmental Elements:** Cherry blossom petals gently fall across the screen
*   **Progressive Difficulty:** Game speed increases with each level while maintaining the serene atmosphere
*   **Organic Visual Design:** Snake segments have a soft, natural look with subtle size variation and gentle color shifts

## 🎮 How to Play

Guide the mystical forest spirit through the enchanted meadow, collecting magical fruits while avoiding collisions. Watch your spirit energy and aim for the highest score!

### Controls

*   **Arrow Keys (↑, ↓, ←, →) or WASD:** Control the forest spirit's direction
*   The spirit cannot reverse direction instantly - plan your moves carefully!

### Gameplay

1.  **Collect Magical Fruits:**
    *   🌺 **Regular Pink Berries:** Increase your score (10 × level) and grow the spirit
    *   ⭐ **Special Star Fruits:** Rare purple stars that restore 20% spirit energy and give bonus points (25 × level)
2.  **Manage Spirit Energy:**
    *   Your energy gradually decreases over time
    *   When energy reaches 0, the spirit returns to rest (game over)
    *   Special star fruits help restore your energy
3.  **Level Progression:**
    *   Level up every 50 points
    *   Each level increases game speed and score multipliers
    *   Magical particles celebrate your achievements
4.  **Avoid Collisions:**
    *   Don't hit the forest boundaries
    *   Don't let the spirit touch its own body
5.  **Enjoy the Atmosphere:** Watch cherry blossom petals drift by and grass patches sway gently

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1.  Clone this repository or download the `index.html` file
2.  Open the `index.html` file in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

No build steps or additional dependencies required! The entire game is contained in a single HTML file.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

*   **HTML5:** Game structure and Canvas element for rendering
*   **CSS3:** Ghibli-inspired styling with:
    *   Soft gradient backgrounds and organic color schemes
    *   Floating cloud animations with CSS keyframes
    *   Custom fonts (Kalam) for whimsical typography
    *   Glassmorphism effects and subtle shadows
*   **JavaScript (ES6+):** Complete game engine including:
    *   **Canvas API:** Rendering the spirit, magical fruits, particles, and environmental effects
    *   Game state management and collision detection
    *   Particle system for magical effects
    *   Smooth animation loops with `requestAnimationFrame`

## 🌸 Design Philosophy

This game captures the essence of Studio Ghibli films through:
- **Soft, Natural Colors:** Pastels and earth tones create a gentle, dreamy atmosphere
- **Organic Movement:** Cherry blossom petals float naturally, grass sways softly
- **Magical Interactions:** Every action creates beautiful particle effects
- **Peaceful Gameplay:** Despite being a Snake game, the focus is on beauty and tranquility
